# India's Defence, Decoded: series plan (draft)

A GyaanXstudios series for a general audience: one defence system or idea per episode, explained with strong animation and verified facts. There's no exam framing, but every episode keeps the channel's principles: explain why, tell a story, connect the dots, use concrete dates, attribute every claim, and stay neutral on debates.

## Look: "Blueprint"
- Deep navy blueprint paper with a fine grid; cyan and white line drawings that draw themselves on; an amber highlight used only for the "Remember it" moment.
- Systems appear as stylised blueprint silhouettes and flight-path diagrams, never engineering drawings.
- **Recurring world: the blueprint wall.** Each episode pins its system as a card, so the wall fills up across the series.
- **Sound:** a low synth pulse bed, radar pings, a "pin" sound when the card lands.
- **Maps:** India in the official Survey of India depiction only. Other regions are schematic, with no boundaries.

## Format (about 8 to 9 minutes, English and Hindi audio tracks)
1. Cold open: a real, dated moment
2. Identity card
3. How it works, in plain words
4. Scale: comparisons people can picture
5. The story so far, as a timeline
6. Why it matters
7. The debate, on equal cards (where one exists)
8. Global cousins
9. Remember it: three words
10. What's next, then the subscribe line

## Rules
- Public facts only: official DRDO, Defence Ministry and PIB statements, published specifications, and reported procurement labelled as reports.
- No design or engineering detail beyond what is officially public; no targeting or deployment specifics.
- No graphic imagery of strikes or casualties.
- No real likenesses: officials appear as generic figures with nameplates.
- No logos or flags.
- Indian and official sources first (the same order as the World Affairs series).
